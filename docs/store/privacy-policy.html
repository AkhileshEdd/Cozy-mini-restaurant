Privacy Policy

Cozy Mini Restaurant · Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy explains what information the Cozy Mini Restaurant game ("the game", "we") uses and why. The game has no accounts, and we do not run servers that receive your personal data.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, café upgrades, decorations, settings and whether you bought "Remove ads") is saved in a file on your device. It is not sent to us.

Backup to your Google account

On Android, the game allows Android Backup. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android copies the game's save file to your Google account so your progress comes back when you reinstall the game or move to a new phone. This backup is stored by Google under your account, and you control it in your phone's settings. We cannot access it.

Advertising

The game shows ads using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type, operating system, language, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states), the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time from Pause → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Learn more about how Google uses information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is handled by Google Play. Google processes your payment details; we never see them. The game only receives confirmation that the purchase was made so it can turn off ads, and it checks this with Google Play on your device.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Data sharing and retention

We do not sell your information and do not share it with anyone except as described above (Google AdMob for ads, Google Play for purchases, and Android Backup if you use it). Uninstalling the game deletes its data from your device.

Changes
